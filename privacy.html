Privacy policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

IIITD Black Market is a student-run noticeboard where IIIT-Delhi students buy, sell, rent and give away things on campus. It is not run by, endorsed by or affiliated with IIIT-Delhi. This page explains what information the site handles and why. It is written in plain language on purpose.

What we collect

  • Sign-in information. When you choose "Continue with IIITD Google", Google tells us your name and your institute email address (an @iiitd.ac.in address is required). We do not receive your Google password, your contacts, your files or your calendar.
  • What you add to the site. Your display name and optional batch, listings (title, description, price, pickup area, photos), saved items, messages and photos you send in chats, offers, and any reports or blocks you make.
  • Notifications. Short alerts such as "New message about …" so you can find your conversations.
  • Technical data. Like any website, the companies that host the site (see below) may keep standard server logs such as IP address and browser type. We do not run advertising or analytics trackers.

How we use it

  • To let you sign in and to check that you belong to the IIIT-Delhi community.
  • To run the marketplace: show your listings, deliver your messages, notify you.
  • To approve current students automatically (see below), and to keep it safe: admins review everyone else, plus reports and abuse, and can remove content or suspend accounts.

We do not sell your information and we do not show ads.

Automatic approval of students

IIIT-Delhi publishes the roll numbers of current B.Tech students on its public website. To approve those students without waiting for an admin, we keep a private list of only the five-digit codes taken from those roll numbers (joining year and serial number). We do not keep names, programs or any other detail from that page. If your institute address ends in one of those codes you are approved automatically; otherwise an admin reviews your request. The list is visible only to admins and is refreshed once a semester.

Who can see what

  • Other approved members can see your display name, optional batch and listings. They cannot see your email address.
  • Chats are visible only to the two people in the conversation and, if a message is reported, to the admins reviewing the report.
  • Admins can see members' institute email addresses, the approval list and reports.
  • Photos are stored in private storage and shown through short-lived links to approved members. Photos are resized in your browser before upload, which also removes hidden camera data such as location.
  • Anyone who has not signed in and been approved sees none of this.

Services that handle data for us

  • Google provides sign-in.
  • Supabase hosts the database, sign-in sessions and file storage (project region: Mumbai, India).
  • GitHub Pages serves the website itself.
  • Google Fonts supplies the site's typefaces, so your browser requests them from Google when a page loads.

These providers process data under their own privacy terms. We share information with no one else unless the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Listings are hidden from others after 30 days unless the seller renews them, and you can delete your own listings at any time. Account details, messages and reports are kept while your account exists so conversations and moderation records stay intact. To have your account and data removed, ask the operators (contact below) and we will delete or anonymise it, except where we need to keep a record of a report or a safety problem.

Your choices

  • Edit your display name and batch on your profile page, and delete or edit your listings.
  • Block people and report listings or messages.
  • Ask for a copy of your data or for deletion by contacting us.
  • Revoke the site's access to your Google sign-in at any time in your Google account's security settings.

Security

The site uses encrypted connections, per-user access rules in the database and private file storage. No online service is perfectly secure, so please do not put sensitive personal details (phone numbers, room numbers, ID numbers) in listings or public descriptions.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes we will update the date above. Questions or requests: contact the operators through the GitHub profile github.com/AbhikRao, or message a site admin.
